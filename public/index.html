FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Teardowns, rewrites, and the bench are free. No account. No upsell.

Where do my photos and recordings go?

The bench edits photos in your browser. Scans send resized photos and text to Anthropic's Claude. Cloudflare transcribes voice, then sends the transcript to Claude. We don't save uploads or reports in a database. Provider retention policies apply. Read the privacy notice.

Screenshots or individual photos?

Both work. Screenshots let the teardown read your prompts. Individual photos get clearer verdicts and can be edited on the bench.

Does a score guarantee more matches?

No. The score covers photo quality, variety, prompts, and intent. Matches also depend on your area, filters, and timing.

Can I save my report?

Yes. Use Copy report or Save report. Reports stay in this tab. Save yours before refreshing or leaving.
