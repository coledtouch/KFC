Before you upload

Is it really free?

Yes. Scans, rewrites, and the photo bench are free, with no account and no upsell.

Where do my photos go?

The photo bench never uploads anything. When you run a scan, your images are shrunk in your browser, then sent to Anthropic's Claude for that one analysis. Keep Fix Cut does not store your photos or your report.

Screenshots or individual photos?

Either works. Full-profile screenshots let the scan read your prompts for you. Individual photos get sharper per-photo calls and can be fixed on the photo bench.

Does a score guarantee more matches?

No. The score measures what the scan can see: photo quality, variety, prompts, and clarity of intent. Matches also depend on your area, your filters, and timing.
